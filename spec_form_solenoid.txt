================================================================================
PRODUCT TECHNICAL SPECIFICATION: PNEUMATIC SOLENOID VALVE (NAMUR)
================================================================================

1. GENERAL & DESIGN INFORMATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Manufacturer:                 ASCO / Emerson Automation Solutions (or Festo)
Series / Model:               Series 327 (Direct Acting) / VOFC (Pilot Operated)
Valve Function:               3/2 Way or 5/2 Way (Convertible via plate/u-ring)
Actuation Type:               Electrical / Solenoid with Manual Override
Mounting Interface:           NAMUR Standard (VDI/VDE 3845) for direct actuator mount

2. PNEUMATIC & FLUID PARAMETERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Operating Medium:             Filtered (50 µm), lubricated or non-lubricated air/inert gas
Flow Rate (Qn / Cv):          950 l/min (Cv = 0.95)
Operating Pressure Min:       0.0 bar (Direct acting) / 2.0 bar (Pilot operated)
Operating Pressure Max:       10.0 bar (145 psi)
Exhaust Port Connections:     G 1/4" or 1/4" NPT
Pilot Air Ports:              G 1/4" with NAMUR interface

3. ELECTRICAL SPECIFICATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Nominal Voltage:              24 V DC (Optional: 110 V AC, 230 V AC)
Power Consumption:            1.8 W (Low Power execution for DCS/PLC)
Duty Cycle:                   100% ED (Continuous Duty)
Electrical Connection:        M20x1.5 Cable Gland / 1/2" NPT Conduit / DIN Plug
Insulation Class:             Class H (High temperature resistance)

4. MATERIALS & ENVIRONMENTAL RESISTANCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Body Material:                Anodized Aluminum / Stainless Steel 316L (for H2S)
Solenoid Enclosure:           Stainless Steel / Epoxy Coated Aluminum
Internal Parts:               Stainless Steel 304 / 316
Dynamic Seal Material:        FKM (Viton) / Fluorosilicone (for sour gas & H2S)
Ambient Temperature Range:    -40°C to +80°C (Standard) / -60°C to +60°C (Arctic)

5. CERTIFICATION & SAFETY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Enclosure Protection:         IP66 / IP67 / NEMA 4, 4X, 7, 9
Explosion Protection:         ATEX / IECEx: Ex d IIC T6 Gb (Flameproof) / Ex ia (Intrinsic Safety)
Safety Integrity Level:       SIL 3 Capable (IEC 61508)
